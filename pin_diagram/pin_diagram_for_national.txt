**Important Notes:**

*   **Power:** Ensure your power supply (battery pack) can handle the current draw of the motors and other components. The L298N handles motor power, while the Nano/SENSORS need a stable 5V (or 3.3V for Nano/MPU6050). If using a buck converter, connect its output 5V/GND to the Nano's 5V/GND pins and sensor VCC/GND.
*   **Common Ground:** It's crucial that the ground of your motor battery pack is connected to the ground of your Nano's power supply. This is the reference point for all signals.
*   **Signal Voltage:** The Nano operates at 5V logic. Most sensors (including HC-SR04) are 5V tolerant. The MPU6050 typically runs at 3.3V, so connecting it to the Nano's 3.3V pin is correct. Be cautious if mixing 3.3V and 5V devices.
*   **L298N Connections:** The specific connections for OUT1/OUT2 depend on your motor and how you want it to rotate. Swapping them reverses the motor direction.
*   **Servo Power:** If your servo draws significant current, consider powering it from the battery pack directly (VCC/GND) rather than through the Nano, to avoid brownouts. Connect the signal wire to D11.
